# Homelab OS Community Guidelines & Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the **Homelab OS** project and community a welcoming, constructive, and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background, technical experience, identity, or setup.

---

## Community Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
* **Constructive Collaboration:** Focusing on solving technical issues, providing clear reproduction steps, and sharing helpful homelab configurations.
* **Respectful Communication:** Being considerate of differing viewpoints, hardware constraints, and experience levels.
* **Empathy & Kindness:** Helping new self-hosters and welcoming community members asking beginner questions.
* **Graceful Feedback:** Accepting and delivering feedback constructively and gracefully.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
* Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments.
* Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning language.
* Publishing others' private information (such as IP addresses, credentials, or personal contact info) without explicit permission.
* Disruptive or unwelcome behavior in issue threads or pull requests.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate.

If you encounter or witness unacceptable behavior:
1. Contact the project maintainers directly via email at: `brijeshy9653@gmail.com`.
2. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, code, or pull requests that do not adhere to these community guidelines.
